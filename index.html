Frequently Asked Questions

Everything You Need to Know

Have more questions? Ping us directly on WhatsApp for an immediate response.

How fast can my project be delivered?

Most projects ship within 10 to 14 business days from kickoff. Larger full-stack applications or custom platforms typically take 3 to 4 weeks with weekly milestones and demo deployments.

How does the Monthly Retainer vs 100% Website Buyout work?

Normally you can pay a low monthly retainer (e.g., ₹1,500/mo for Basic) for all-inclusive hosting, updates, and lifetime management. If you ever decide you don't want to keep paying monthly, you can buy out the entire website outright for ₹7,000 total (for Basic). After buying out the website, 100% of the code is yours, and ongoing repairs or management drops to just ₹500/month!

Is there a minimum rental period?

Yes. Basic has a 5-month minimum, and Professional and Premium have a 4-month minimum. This covers the cost of designing and building your custom website. After the minimum period, you can cancel anytime with 30 days’ notice.

Can I cancel during the minimum period?

Rent for the minimum period remains payable, so cancelling early means the remaining months are due. Alternatively, you can switch to owning at any time by paying the full Own price for your package.

Can I buy during the minimum period?

Yes. Pay the full Own price and the commitment ends. Rent already paid isn’t credited toward the purchase.

What if I miss a payment?

You’ll get a reminder and a 7-day grace period. After that, the site goes offline until the pending amount is paid. Missed months during the minimum period remain payable.

Do I own 100% of the code and website?

Yes, absolutely. Once the project buyout is settled, 100% intellectual property, repository access, and hosting credentials belong entirely to you with zero lock-in.

How do we communicate during the build?

Directly via a dedicated WhatsApp / Telegram group or Slack channel with our lead engineer and designer. No middlemen, no delayed ticketing systems.
